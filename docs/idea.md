# Idea: meeting-room booking for a coworking space

*The starting point for Lab 1. Deliberately short and incomplete: turning it into a reviewable plan is the exercise.*

---

From the community manager of a coworking space:

> We have three meeting rooms: a small one for 4 people, a medium one for 8, and a large one for 14 with a projector. Right now members book them on a shared spreadsheet and we keep getting double bookings and arguments.
>
> I'd like a simple web page where members can see which rooms are free today and this week, and book a slot. Bookings are in 30-minute steps, between 08:00 and 20:00. A member shouldn't be able to hog a room all day.
>
> It has to work on a phone. We don't want logins for now; people just type their name.
>
> If it works, we'd later want recurring bookings and maybe a screen next to each room showing what's on.
